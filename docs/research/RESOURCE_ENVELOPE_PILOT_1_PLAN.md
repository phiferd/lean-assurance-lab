## Question and fixed scope

Measure the observed resource envelope for two valid term families at six
prespecified sizes on two supported implementations. The fixed matrix remains
two families × six sizes × two observers. Record every attempted size and stop
increasing size only within the affected family/observer cell after its bound
process safety limit is observed; continue the remaining cells when controls
remain valid. Intentional per-cell limit observations are data. Supervisor,
accounting, timeout, monitor, or cleanup failures are nonterminal engineering
failures that require preservation, repair, and rerun within this item.

The result describes this finite envelope only. It supports no asymptotic,
general speed, or universal memory claim.
