# Test whether existing methods cover the new recursor failures

**What did we find?** Upstream reports describe two Nanoda failures involving
imported recursion data, with merged fixes and one shared Arena regression.
The Lab has not tested whether its earlier methods would detect them.
**Is it interesting?** Yes: both reports concern acceptance of invalid proofs,
and a miss could expose a reusable gap in our methods. **Does it require more
work?** Compare the unchanged pre-disclosure assets with exact faulty/fixed
versions, then consider a separate transfer screen on new checkers.

Evidence class: E0. Both items below are PLANNED and unstarted. This plan
authorizes preparation only after canonical selection; no execution starts in
the 2026-10-24 strategy reassessment.

## E0-RECURSOR-HISTORICAL-GAP-1

Targets: Nanoda issue #60 / fix #63 (nested recursor field count) and issue #64 /
fix #65 (relocated recursor escaping validation). These are independently
discovered historical faults, not blind holdouts: the mechanisms are already
known to this team. Upstream reproduction claims remain unverified locally.

Before any launch, bind a fixed inventory of Lab tests, mutation operators,
model checks, generators, selection rules and seeds available at pre-disclosure
commit `3a116a7a19a149bb8d34b5d5a022f00b8bd7dff0`. Record whether each asset
actually covers metadata consistency, block placement and complete validation
of every imported recursor. Bind exact faulty and fixed source revisions from
the upstream fixes, public-entrypoint adapters, expected semantics, controls and
runtime identities. Establish source/format compatibility without changing a
detector. A source mapping is a coverage hypothesis, not demonstrated detection.

Register one trial per fault before execution. First use only the frozen assets
and their recorded selection rules; do not read or import published witness
bytes or Arena #285 into that detector set. Retain exact exposure provenance,
including the known bug descriptions. Report HIT, MISS or NOT_APPLICABLE per
asset with executable evidence and the E0 trial's SIGNAL/NO_SIGNAL/INCONCLUSIVE
classification. A miss without an eligible input is not a measured detection
failure. No aggregate fault-detection rate from two selected bugs.

If the frozen assets miss, finish with a concrete gap and a separately proposed
general invariant or input transformation. Do not retune the detector and then
claim prior discovery. Later use of published witnesses is a separately labeled
reproduction control; newly designed checks are informed development. Test any
generalization prospectively on a different, frozen semantic boundary, with an
independent expected-result oracle and valid controls. No known case supplies
independent-transfer credit or changes the existing holdout intake gate.

Use existing E0 ledger, raw retention and campaign handoff. Repair engineering
failures in the same item; no attempt caps, per-trial full suite or routine
independent closure review. Shared tooling edits retain their full regressions.
Complete one two-fault campaign, select a successor once and stop.

## E0-NEW-CHECKER-RECURSOR-TRANSFER-1

Separate PLANNED screen: use Arena's Pestka and Kefir integrations to test one
previously unexamined imported-recursor invariant selected from the historical
audit, with invalid/valid pairs and a semantic oracle independent of agreement.
Before promotion, pin working checker recipes, source revisions and supported
format/fragment; perform a narrow source/literature reuse check and exclude
overlap with existing Arena cases. Record unsupported/declined cases separately.
Choose the exact boundary and cases before execution. Checker diversity alone
is not oracle independence and does not satisfy TRANSFER-HOLDOUT-INTAKE-1.
No positive-only agreement matrix, whole-Arena benchmark or new framework.

Keep the universe-test candidate DEFERRED until a current source inventory shows
distinct protection and a concrete consumer benefit justifies its E1 cost.
Read existing maintainer guidance; seeking a response or submitting anything
requires exact external-action authorization. A new recursor regression does
not itself establish that the universe test is redundant. No external write.
