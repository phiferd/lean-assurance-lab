## Question and value

For the exact supported NDJSON fragment and frozen cohort selected by pilot 1,
does a permitted nonidentity record representation change either bound
checker's semantic acceptance of the same declarations and expression graph?
The primary relation is original versus variant within each checker. Agreement
between checkers is never semantic authority.

A successful continuation produces executed, auditable original/variant pairs,
a complete fixed observation matrix and reusable transformation/preservation
tooling. This remains worth pursuing because pilot 1 generated no scientific
byte and therefore supplied no evidence for or against the hypothesis; its
failure was confined to process supervision.

## Finite resource envelope without a tooling-attempt counter

All continuation work shares 720 cumulative active minutes. Paired UTC and
monotonic intervals charge implementation, diagnosis, repair, generation,
execution and closure; breaks do not consume time. This larger envelope is a
resource safeguard, not a target and not a license to manufacture activity.

- There is no per-invocation tooling-test attempt cap. Focused regressions may
  be rerun as needed within the aggregate active-time and process-time ceilings.
  Every invocation and result is still logged.
- At most 24 read-only source/setup requests and eight build reservations, each
  build at most 600 seconds.
- Tooling and scientific child processes each have an explicit timeout of at
  most 120 seconds during validation and 30 seconds in the frozen checker
  matrix. Combined child-process wall time is at most 14,400 seconds.
- Generated scientific scope remains at most two originals, two transformations,
  four variants and the inherited twelve-cell maximum matrix. Payload limits
  remain 1 MiB and 20,000 records per artifact and 60 MiB retained total.
- Exactly the inherited two profiles may run. At most 120 checker process
  attempts are available, with at most twelve in the initial matrix and the
  remainder reserved solely for exact fixed-pair diagnosis or confirmation.
- The production memory ceiling remains 2 GiB per process. The host-native
  supervisor must demonstrate observation, group termination and cleanup on
  bounded synthetic controls before scientific launch. A sampled RSS ceiling is
  reported as such; it is not represented as an operating-system hard limit.
- Zero proof search, kernel-source mutation, production-checker edit, external
  research write or assurance-milestone advance.

The item stops successfully only after at least one nonidentity,
preservation-passing pair is tested on both profiles with compatible baselines
and every frozen matrix cell has a recorded disposition. An unsuccessful stop
requires mechanical evidence that the fixed goal is not achievable with the
available host/toolchains, or a review showing that a successful result has
lost most of its expected shared value. The constitutional finite envelope is
also a hard bound, but ordinary tooling defects remain repair work rather than
a scientific result.

## Phase 1: host-native supervision first

Recover the final pilot-1 code by exact Git object identity into the live tree,
then isolate supervision from transformation and generation. Before any variant
is generated:

1. reproduce the attempt-8 failure with receipts printed even on assertion
   failure;
2. identify the actual child exit/monitor state rather than infer it from test
   duration;
3. add the smallest regression for the cause;
4. validate a normal exit, a nonzero exit, timeout of a process group, memory
   breach observation and cleanup, including a child process in the group; and
5. bind the exact implementation/test hashes and raw receipts in a tooling
   revision.

Prefer direct `subprocess.Popen(..., start_new_session=True)` plus host-supported
`ps` observation. Do not depend on Linux-only `RLIMIT_AS` or GNU `time`. A
monitor error, absent sample, unproven process-group cleanup or missing receipt
fails closed. Synthetic controls are tooling tests, not checker attempts.

## Phase 2: transformation and preservation gate

Recover the transformer and independent auditor from the bound snapshot, then
run the frozen positive, adversarial, determinism and identity-round-trip cases.
The auditor must fail closed on unknown tags, extra/missing records, duplicate or
non-contiguous identifiers, unresolved references and changed semantic fields.
It must preserve raw `imax`/`max`, ordered parameters, binders, names,
declarations, dependencies and assumptions. Transformer round-trip is not the
preservation oracle.

Only after both focused gates pass may the exact four variants be generated.
Record byte hashes, transformation reports and audit reports. Identity or
ineligible variants remain retained with explicit dispositions and never count
as successful metamorphic comparisons.

## Phase 3: generated-byte and checker-launch gate
