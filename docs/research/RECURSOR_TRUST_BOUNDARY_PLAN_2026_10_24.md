# Defensive validation of imported recursor metadata

**What did we find?** Earlier work mutated imported recursor metadata, but the
reviewed history does not demonstrate coverage of field-count or placement
failures. **Is it interesting?** Yes: imported information may reach trusted
reduction or type inference through a path that skips the expected check.
**Does it require more work?** Measure earlier coverage, then test an explicitly
informed boundary-mutation strategy and select a distinct transfer target.

This defensive regression plan is the prospective successor to
[the initial October 24 plan](RECURSOR_HISTORICAL_GAP_AND_TRANSFER_PLAN.md).
The [historical companion](../../results/research/strategy-reassessment-2026-10-24/recursor-history-companion.md)
records counterevidence and limits. All three items below are PLANNED and
unstarted; canonical selection precedes preparation or execution. The separate
merged-Lean replay remains READY. No frozen result or milestone gate changes.

## Small source inventory, shared across the three items

Start with Nanoda and the already studied Kiota path. For each relevant
recursor field or relationship, record: pinned source location; public input
origin; stored representation; validation or safe reconstruction; installation
path; and consumer in reduction, definitional equality (equality by computation),
or type inference. Include ordinary and restored recursors, block/dependency
ordering, and any alternate insertion or lookup path actually present in source.
Record unknown paths explicitly. This is one compact table, not a whole-codebase
audit or a new catalog/schema.

The obligation is that every consumer sees validated or safely reconstructed
information. Rejection of every modified serialized field is not a universal
oracle: ignored or replaced redundant metadata can legitimately be accepted.
Map con-leche and lean4lean analogues by the same obligation before choosing
transfer executions; add Pestka/Kefir where supported. Pin source and format
support rather than assuming identical architectures or accepting consensus as
semantic authority.

## E0-RECURSOR-BOUNDARY-MUTATION-1: informed retrospective development

This separate experiment asks whether a reusable defensive validation strategy
can detect metadata-integrity failures on the frozen pre-repair targets. Knowledge of the faults
is declared; even a newly generated witness is not independent discovery.

Derive transformations from the inventory's semantic obligations. Candidate
families are numeric metadata (including field counts), rule bodies, constructor
ownership/associations, and declaration placement. Freeze family selection,
parameters, seed provenance, ordering and oracle before inspecting generated
outcomes. Use valid preexisting exports; exclude published reproduction artifacts,
exact reproduction-derived parameters and report-specific branching in the generator.
Document why each chosen family follows from an obligation across checkers.
Unselected or unsupported families receive an explicit reason, not a coverage
claim. Freeze the chosen finite scientific domain; counts are observations,
not engineering stopping caps.

Mutate imported data rather than removing internal checks. Preserve framing,
references and unrelated structure so parser failures do not masquerade as
semantic rejection. Placement transformations must respect the format's actual
dependency/installation contract; include both unchanged controls and valid
relationship-preserving variants. Identify the downstream consumer: altered
metadata must be observable in a reduction, typing or equality judgment, rather
than merely present in the environment.

For every alleged violation, establish the expected rejection independently of
checker agreement and distinguish contract rejection from a metadata-integrity
failure that affects a trusted judgment. Compare the same generated case on
pre-repair and repaired revisions with successful controls. A version difference
alone is not a semantic oracle. Retain all attempts, unsupported cases and
engineering failures. Published reproduction inputs may
later be separate labeled reproduction controls, never generator inputs or
evidence of prior capability. Close this bounded experiment before transfer;
no known target earns independent holdout credit.

con-leche and lean4lean obligations, plus supported Pestka/Kefir opportunities.

feasible checker profile(s); do not run every checker just to fill a matrix.

independent oracle. Inspect existing Arena coverage and source reuse first.
Report unsupported or safely reconstructed paths separately from tested
rejection. Checker diversity and shared lineage do not establish oracle
independence; existing TRANSFER-HOLDOUT-INTAKE-1 custody gates still apply.

At closure of a consequential trust-boundary finding in this frontier, the
existing strategic comparison must include a compact disposition for analogous
obligations in other relevant checkers: scheduled investigation, existing
evidence of coverage, unsupported path, or deferral with a concrete reason.
Link source/evidence and select only the highest-value next experiment. This
applies the existing closure process; it introduces no standing review task,
blanket audit, automatic launch or external submission.
