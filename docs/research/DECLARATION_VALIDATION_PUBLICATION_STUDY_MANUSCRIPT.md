## 1. Problem and Motivation

The study asks whether a deliberately bounded set of ordinary-declaration
validation obligations can be supported by admissible normative authority and,
for the obligations that survive that test, whether existing or directed
negative tests isolate the exact semantic predicate rather than merely reaching
related code or eliciting some rejection.

## 2. Bounded Lean Trust-Boundary Model

The modeled boundary is the pinned M8/M9 ordinary-declaration contract slice.
It distinguishes normative kernel-declaration validity from empirical contract
scenarios, observer behavior, reconstruction policy, and implementation detail.
The model, stable identities, and their historical bindings remain external to
this manuscript and are not redefined here.

## 4. Normative Authority Methodology

### Frozen Research Questions

1. **RQ1 — Authority Qualification.** For the preregistered M10-ready cohort,
   which exact obligations, if any, can be established for the pinned modeled
   judgment from version-matched, independently admissible normative
   documentation or mechanized results?
2. **RQ2 — Existing Isolated-Negative Coverage.** For the frozen established
   denominator, which obligations have an existing Arena case satisfying the
   complete M10 isolated-negative contract rather than merely reaching related
   checker code or being rejected for some reason?
3. **RQ3 — Relationship to Coarse Indicators.** Where do exact source-line
   coverage and mutation-kill indications agree or disagree with
   obligation-sensitive isolated-negative coverage? These indicators remain
   secondary observations, not semantic authority or a substitute denominator.
4. **RQ4 — Directed Synthesis.** For established obligations lacking existing
   isolated coverage, how often can the frozen tiered strategy construct a
   content-bound negative artifact satisfying `Reach(x) AND NOT P(x)`, a
   matched positive control, and complete competing-obligation analysis within
   the preregistered budget?
5. **RQ5 — Cross-Validator and Ecosystem Value.** Do exact generated or newly
   isolated cases expose attributed implementation differences, confirm
   consistent handling, strengthen shared Arena coverage, or support an
   evidence-based recommendation of no external action?

Authority qualification follows the frozen separation between source
discovery, source approval, claim-scope repair, and candidate adjudication.
Only an `ESTABLISHED` disposition satisfying the unchanged M10 conjunction can
enter the strict primary denominator. Provisional and unresolved candidates
remain visible but non-normative.

## 5. Obligation-Sensitive Negative Coverage

Coverage is defined per frozen obligation `P` by the complete M10 isolation
contract, including a reachable negative witness, the exact target violation,
a matched positive control, and competing-obligation analysis. Line coverage,
mutation kills, checker agreement, and rejection alone do not establish this
form of semantic coverage.

## 6. Experimental Design

Execution is sequentially gated: authority adjudication precedes mechanical
denominator freeze; baseline and comparator classification precedes directed
synthesis; targets, strategies, controls, prior inputs, and budgets are frozen
before generation. The exact corpus, observer set, budgets, and stop rules are
read from canonical preregistered artifacts. Negative, null, empty-denominator,
and bounded-exhaustion outcomes remain valid results.

## 8. Threats to Validity

The frozen threats include incomplete or version-mismatched normative
documentation, a small or empty denominator, bounded representability through
the pinned Arena pipeline, misattribution among competing obligations,
observer-specific implementation behavior, and the inability of coarse
indicators to substitute for semantic evidence. Claim tiers constrain reporting
scope rather than changing denominator membership.
