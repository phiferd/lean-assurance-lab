Arena #176, #181 and #182 have retained merge evidence. This review additionally
verified that [Arena #206](https://github.com/leanprover/lean-kernel-arena/pull/206)

awaiting review and four local Nanoda candidates. The let case demonstrates a
useful sequence: preserve ambiguity, ask a precise question, incorporate the
answer, and contribute an appropriately qualified characterization.

The evidence discipline is a substantial asset. Negative HSBM yield was
retained instead of expanding the sample; implementation policy was separated
from universal semantic authority; conditional proofs retain their assumptions;
frozen observations were not silently reinterpreted against newer sources.
The assurance gate still fails for recorded semantic disagreements. That is a
visible limitation, not a reason to weaken the gate.

The canonical assurance snapshot records 197 materialized tests, three pinned
implementation families, 15 semantic and one parse-behavior disagreement
artifacts, and two pending survivors included in the modeled population.
Those are input-bound measurements, not counts of distinct current bugs and
not a proof of kernel correctness. Independent transfer remains limited by
the absent fresh input/custody or functioning independent-target package.

## Where expected value has weakened

Recent work concentrates on Nanoda internal preventive tests. These are valid
contributions, but four tested local drafts are an intermediate result rather
than four adopted ecosystem improvements. Fresh reads of
[Nanoda #32](https://github.com/ammkrn/nanoda_lib/pull/32) and
[#33](https://github.com/ammkrn/nanoda_lib/pull/33) found both OPEN at their
unchanged heads; the combined discussion endpoints returned no entries.
Check-run endpoints were not queried. This establishes neither maintainer
capacity nor rejection. Further unspecified Nanoda test discovery should have
a lower priority while these assets await feedback.

The selected one-thread question is executable and bounded, but its immediate
benefit is primarily explaining one operational boundary of a pinned historical
mutant. A deterministic trigger is not required before its source assessment;
it is required before a later experiment. The last closure review correctly
recognized diminishing returns from more Nanoda drafts, but compared the
one-thread reserve chiefly with blocked theorem/transfer routes. Being the only
READY entry does not establish the best project-wide opportunity.

Visibility has also drifted in ways worth correcting now. The contribution
ledger missed #206's merge. The live Unresolved Problems paragraph incorrectly
placed pending survivors outside the modeled denominator. The Kiota waiting
entry still grouped old k/type/cidx accepts into a potential report even though
the completed import-contract review already documents k recomputation and a
newer cidx guard. These are status corrections; historical results, disagreement
counts, authority decisions and assurance gates remain unchanged.

There is no reliable end-to-end cost comparison between methods: the generated
project review notes absent per-task model-token accounting. Cheap checker
runtime does not establish cheap investigation. No efficiency or prevalence
claim is justified by the small fixed HSBM pilot or by counts of closed items.
