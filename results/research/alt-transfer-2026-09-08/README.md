## Reuse decision

Reuse the controls from the bounded methods review; stop scientific execution
until suitable inputs exist. [Just et al.](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~mernst/pubs/mutation-effectiveness-fse2014.pdf)
motivate isolating faults and controlling coverage. The size-controlled findings
of [Papadakis et al.](https://orbilu.uni.lu/bitstream/10993/34950/1/ICSE-main18b%20%281%29.pdf)
make it unsafe to infer real-fault effectiveness from a score alone. Future
comparison should report direct outcomes under fixed input and resource budgets.
This is a methodological inference, not an empirical result about Lean.

The [versioned Lean manual](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/4.34.0-rc2/ValidatingProofs/)
also motivates keeping artifact interpretation, checker implementation and
statement correspondence distinct. Its retrieval here grants no new authority
to the declaration-validation catalog.

[Sources](sources.json) preserve decisions, locators, versions and fetched-byte
identities. [Retrieval records](retrievals.json) retain failed access, including
an institutional PDF certificate failure and the separately hashed author
preprint. The review used four queries, five source groups and one pinned code
inspection; it was not an exhaustive survey. No verification claim in an
