## Feasibility and methodological limits

Independent local strategy and source-feasibility reviews support the choice.
The retained parser rejects duplicate Name/Level/Expr records and requires
contiguous IDs, so arbitrary sparse renumbering and duplication are poor default
relations. Prefer candidate topological reorderings with typed reference
rewriting and legal record-kind interleaving, initially inside declaration-free
blocks. Preserve declaration order/grouping and every semantic field. These
are planning observations; exact format legality and sources must be rebound
in the pilot, and no transformation or cohort was implemented or frozen here.

Retained official-Lean and Nanoda profiles provide a local feasibility path;
they are not current-upstream observations or new binary attestations. The
source/reuse phase must choose exact supported profiles, distinguish raw parser
errors/panics from semantic rejection, and bind executable/configuration identities.
A transformation and its own inverse are insufficient independent evidence.
Changed-object negative controls must fail the preservation audit.
Final independent review also added a non-vacuity gate: identity/inapplicable
transformations remain visible but cannot count as eligible comparisons. A
successful executed pilot needs a nonidentity preservation-passing pair tested
on both profiles with compatible baselines.

The frozen initial matrix is capped at 96 checker attempts within a total cap
of 120, retaining repair/confirmation capacity. The proposal's full twelve-by-four
variant matrix would otherwise consume every launch before a rerun. Cohort,
transformations, eligibility and generation failures remain visible after freeze;
no replacing difficult cases, hidden test invocations or budget reset.

Focused primary-source literature/reuse refresh is a bounded entry phase, not
claimed complete by this strategy review. The dated methods map remains
background; its old no-active-item statement is not the current queue. This
review neither claims method novelty nor promotes a representation difference
to kernel unsoundness.
