# Historical-fault transfer campaign

**What did we find?** The unchanged 90-file compact sample did not distinguish
the affected and fixed official Lean binaries for the nested-unused-parameter
fault. It produced two Nanoda differences, but both are the already-known
possibly-Prop projection-policy distinction rather than evidence of the target
projection-structure identity fault. Across both trials, 360 supervised
executions completed over 180 paired observations with no compatibility,
timeout or cleanup boundary.

**Is it interesting?** Yes, negatively. The sample's useful seeded-fault
coverage did not transfer to either target historical fault. The Nanoda source
review also prevented two real but unrelated verdict changes from being counted
as target coverage.

**Does it require more work?** No result-driven work follows. Do not recommend a
PR or E1 confirmation from this campaign. The next project queue item is a
separate planning decision.

The official trial closes `NO_SIGNAL`. The Nanoda trial closes `SIGNAL` because
E0 records raw differing verdicts before causal interpretation; its diagnostic
review classifies those differences as an unrelated known profile distinction.
This result is limited to the exact four retained binaries and the fixed sample.
